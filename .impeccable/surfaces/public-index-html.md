# Checker page (/)

Mode: operate. Visitor: small-business owner or marketer, arriving pre-filled from a cold email (/?d=) or empty from search, equally. Task: read their own result, know the first fix.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The result is a posted inspection card, the grade every business owner already reads in a shop window. Refuses the category default of a score ring over a 2x2 grid of identical cards.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite + lime. Pale stone ground, white card in a thick graphite frame with a graphite band label, Public Sans heavy figures. Status is three far-apart solid fills, each with a drawn icon: lime = Good, orange = Check, crimson = Fix needed. The disc and the scale band take the verdict colour. Hairlines elsewhere, no shadows.

STORY: The visitor sees their score and verdict on the card, sees which one thing to fix first, and reads each check in plain words with the raw record on demand.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop two columns: left, headline, one-line lede, input + button, then checks ranked by urgency as a bordered list; right, the inspection card (band, score disc, verdict, one-line meaning, five-band scale, first-fix link, domain and inspection date). Before a check the card shows an uninspected state. Phone: card sits directly under the input, list below.

FORM: Shop-window Grade Card (Impeccable's pick, rank 1 of 7, palette changed by user to graphite + lime). seed:19272d33e54bda78

SIGNATURE: the card is stamped when a result lands: the disc settles from scale 1.1 and a -6deg tilt with the band fill sweeping to the scored segment, once, reduced-motion safe.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
